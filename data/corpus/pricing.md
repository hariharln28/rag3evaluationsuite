# Pricing

Northstar sells monthly and annual subscriptions.

Starter is $499 per month and includes Pulse only. Team is $1,900 per month and includes Pulse plus Helix. Enterprise is $4,800 per month and includes Pulse, Helix, and Atlas.

Atlas can be added to Team for $700 per month. Starter cannot purchase Atlas or Helix.

Annual prepay receives an 18% discount relative to the monthly list price. A 14-day unpaid sandbox is available; after 21 days of sandbox use the workspace is frozen until a paid plan is selected.

Invoice currency is USD. Northstar does not publish per-seat pricing; Team and Enterprise are billed per workspace, not per user.
