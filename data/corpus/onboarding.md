# Onboarding

A typical Team onboarding is 14 days from contract to first production alert. A typical Enterprise onboarding is 6 weeks and includes a dedicated Technical Account Manager.

Sandboxes last 21 days. Sandbox data is destroyed 7 days after freeze if the workspace is not converted to a paid plan.

Northstar professional services are sold in 40-hour blocks at $280 per hour. Implementation of customer-managed keys requires professional services and is not self-serve.

Training workshops are included twice per year for Enterprise and once per year for Team. Starter receives documentation and recorded webinars only.
