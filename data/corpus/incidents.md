# Incident response

Customers report incidents through the in-app reporter or by emailing incidents@northstar.dev. Enterprise customers may also call the Sev-1 bridge number listed in the admin console.

Acknowledgement targets: Sev-1 within 15 minutes, Sev-2 within 1 hour, Sev-3 within 8 support hours.

Public status is published at https://status.northstar.dev. Northstar posts a written postmortem for Sev-1 incidents within 72 hours of resolution.

A Sev-1 is defined as a complete outage of query or ingestion in a residency region, or confirmed unauthorized access to customer data.
