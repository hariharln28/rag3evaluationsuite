# Security and compliance

Northstar maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certification. The latest SOC 2 report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA.

Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Customer encryption keys may be supplied via AWS KMS or GCP Cloud KMS on Enterprise (customer-managed keys).

Single sign-on supports SAML 2.0 and OIDC. SCIM provisioning is included on Team and Enterprise.

Data residency options are United States and European Union. Workspaces cannot split a single environment across both regions.

Northstar does not currently offer HIPAA Business Associate Agreements and does not market the products as a medical-device or PHI platform. PCI cardholder data must not be sent to Pulse, Helix, or Atlas.
