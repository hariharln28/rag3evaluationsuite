# Service levels and support

Uptime SLA is measured monthly on the query API, excluding announced maintenance windows.

Starter has no contractual uptime SLA. Team is credited against a 99.5% monthly uptime target. Enterprise is credited against a 99.9% monthly uptime target.

Service credits are 10% of that month's subscription fee for each 0.1 percentage point below the target, capped at 50% of the monthly fee. Credits are the sole remedy for SLA misses.

Support response targets: Starter email within 24 hours; Team email or chat within 8 hours during support hours; Enterprise 1-hour response for Sev-1 and Sev-2, including phone.

Support hours are 06:00 to 20:00 Pacific Time, Monday through Friday, excluding US federal holidays. Enterprise Sev-1 coverage is 24x7.
