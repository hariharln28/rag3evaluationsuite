# Product limits

Pulse dashboards: Starter 50, Team 500, Enterprise unlimited. Alert rules: Starter 100, Team 2,000, Enterprise unlimited.

Helix online feature keys: Team 5,000, Enterprise 50,000. Point-in-time correct training exports are capped at 200 GB per job on Team and 2 TB on Enterprise.

Atlas semantic domains: Team 12, Enterprise unlimited. Each domain may contain up to 400 canonical metrics.

Log ingest caps: Starter 20 GB per day, Team 200 GB per day, Enterprise 2 TB per day unless a custom contract states otherwise. Overages are billed at $0.12 per GB on Team and $0.08 per GB on Enterprise. Starter overage is not offered; ingest pauses at the cap.
