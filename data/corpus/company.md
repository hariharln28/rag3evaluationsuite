# Northstar Analytics — Company

Northstar Analytics is a private B2B software company that builds observability and feature-platform tools for data teams. It was founded in 2017 in Portland, Oregon by Mara Ellison and Priya Raman.

As of March 2026 the company employs 420 people. Headquarters remain in Portland, with additional offices in Austin, Texas and Dublin, Ireland.

Northstar closed a $90 million Series C in September 2024, led by Horizon Ventures, with participation from Copperline Capital. The company has not announced an IPO and does not trade on any public exchange.

The stated mission is to make operational data trustworthy enough to drive automated decisions. Northstar does not sell advertising products and does not operate a consumer social network.
